# Social-preview specification

## Purpose

Repository and product-site preview for GitHub, Slack, Messages, and other link unfurls.

## Canvas

- 1280 × 640 pixels
- Opaque PNG
- Safe text area: 72px from every edge

## Exact copy

```text
TARMAC
THE ENTERPRISE DELIVERY CONTROL PLANE
From idea to impact. Governed by design.

TRIAGE  →  ARCHITECTURE  →  RELEASE  →  MONITORING
AUTOMATION & CI/CD  ·  COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE
```

## Visual treatment

Dark Night canvas with a faint aligned grid, warm Runway typography, Signal teal delivery line, Beacon decision markers, and restrained Panel surfaces. No aircraft imagery, photography, gradients inside the mark, third-party logos, or invented metrics.

## Accessibility and inspection

Text must remain legible at small preview sizes. Inspect every word after generation. The PNG contains no embedded confidential data and no unsupported performance claim.
