The coordination gap

Delivery does not fail in one tool.
It fails between them.

01

Fragmented evidence

Strategy, work, security, deployment, operations, and value live in systems with different owners and clocks.

02

Implicit governance

Required decisions arrive through institutional memory, manual checklists, and late escalation.

03

Reporting without action

Leaders receive lagging summaries instead of the precise decision that can still change an outcome.

Six capabilities.
One governed loop.

TARMAC does not centralize every decision in the ITPMO. It gives the ITPMO a shared operating rhythm while architects, risk owners, release authorities, sponsors, and Finance retain accountability.

Calculation and caveats

Capacity = initiatives × baseline hours × improvement × hourly cost × confidence. Delay = accelerated initiatives × weeks × weekly value × confidence. Risk = annual exposure × modeled reduction × confidence. Net value subtracts annual platform cost. Avoided cost, redirected spend, realized benefits, and cashable savings require separate evidence.
