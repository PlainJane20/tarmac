# Code of conduct

## Our standard

TARMAC discussions should be rigorous, welcoming, and safe. Participants are expected to show empathy, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on the health of the project and community.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, reviews, discussions, events, and private communication about TARMAC. Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate it.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality and a response proportionate to the behavior and risk.
