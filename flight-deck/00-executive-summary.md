# 00 — Executive summary

## The proposition

TARMAC is the **Enterprise Delivery Control Plane**: a governance and orchestration layer that connects strategic demand, solution architecture, release readiness, production monitoring, automation, and compliance.

Organizations already have systems for tickets, code, pipelines, service management, finance, and reporting. Their recurring problem is the space between those systems: unclear decision rights, manually assembled evidence, late risk discovery, and weak traceability from investment to outcome.

TARMAC makes those seams visible and governable without replacing authoritative execution tools.

## The operating model

Four pillars form a continuous delivery spine:

```text
Triage → Architecture → Release → Monitoring → Triage
```

Two capabilities apply across the full cycle:

```text
Automation & CI/CD
Compliance & Governance
```

Monitoring returns operational health and realized-value evidence to Triage. The result is a portfolio feedback loop rather than a linear project checklist.

## The target users

| User | Decision TARMAC improves |
| --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | Where should intervention or investment change? |
| ITPMO leader | Is portfolio flow aligned to strategy, capacity, risk, and value? |
| Enterprise architect | Is the solution direction sound, reusable, secure, and evidenced? |
| Release authority | Is production risk understood and explicitly accepted? |
| Engineering/operations leader | Which constraint or signal needs action now? |
| Compliance and audit partner | Which policy applied, what evidence supported it, and who decided? |

## Value hypotheses

TARMAC should reduce decision latency, evidence-preparation effort, late dependency discovery, and release ambiguity while increasing traceability and benefit follow-through. These are hypotheses, not claims. Foundation v0.1.0 defines how they will be baselined and measured in a pilot.

## What Foundation v0.1.0 delivers

- a coherent product and acronym story;
- a versioned Flight Deck and architecture catalog;
- an interactive GitHub Pages product experience;
- a transparent, risk-adjusted value calculator;
- brand, governance, metrics, and engineering standards;
- validation and repository workflows; and
- the existing working application and domain model, preserved.

## What it does not claim

Foundation v0.1.0 is not production-ready, certified, employer-approved, or open source. It contains representative experiences and modeled scenarios. Identity, authorization, tenant isolation, integrations, operational reliability, privacy controls, and formal compliance assessment remain roadmap work.

## Conditions for continued investment

Proceed beyond foundation when representative users validate the operating model, a controlled pilot can establish credible baselines, project ownership and license are resolved, and the trust architecture is feasible without creating a new layer of administrative burden.
