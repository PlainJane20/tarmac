# 01 — Product vision

## Vision

Every technology initiative should have one clear, governed route from idea to measurable impact.

## Mission

Modernize the ITPMO with an evidence-backed operating model that connects portfolio decisions, delivery flow, production reality, and realized business value.

## Category

**Enterprise Delivery Control Plane**

TARMAC coordinates decisions and evidence across systems of work. It does not attempt to become another universal project-management, DevOps, ITSM, or analytics suite.

## Product principles

1. **Decisions over dashboards.** Every view clarifies a decision, accountable owner, deadline, or intervention.
2. **Governance in the flow.** Controls are lifecycle behavior, not paperwork reconstructed at the end.
3. **Evidence over confidence theater.** Readiness includes sources, freshness, exceptions, and rationale.
4. **Integrate, do not duplicate.** Native tools retain their authoritative records.
5. **Explain every score.** Method, inputs, confidence, and assumptions remain inspectable.
6. **Production closes the loop.** Monitoring and realized outcomes feed the next triage decision.
7. **Humans remain accountable.** Automation prepares, evaluates, routes, and recommends; authorized people decide.

## Jobs to be done

- When demand exceeds capacity, compare initiatives using consistent, explainable criteria.
- When architecture review begins, assemble complete evidence and reveal unresolved decisions early.
- When a release approaches, show precisely what is passed, blocked, pending, waived, or stale.
- When production signals change, connect the impact to commitments, owners, and portfolio choices.
- When leaders ask what an investment achieved, compare an owned baseline, target, actual, and financial interpretation.

## Product boundaries

TARMAC is not a backlog, code host, pipeline runner, incident system, finance ledger, document repository, or general-purpose BI platform. It links to those systems, normalizes the facts relevant to governance, and owns the cross-system decision record.

## North-star experience

A user opens TARMAC and immediately sees the few decisions that can most improve outcomes today. Selecting one reveals the complete chain from strategic premise through architecture, dependency, evidence, approval, release, production health, and realized value—without hiding uncertainty or forcing manual status reconstruction.

## Success standard

Success is not more data entered into TARMAC. Success is less time reconstructing status and evidence, faster accountable decisions, fewer late surprises, and more benefits reviewed with credible data.
