# 05 — Brand system

The implementation-ready system lives in [`brand/`](../brand/README.md).

## Brand idea

**Clarity before movement.** TARMAC makes complex enterprise delivery understandable, governable, and ready to move.

## Positioning

For ITPMO and technology leaders coordinating delivery across many teams and tools, TARMAC is an Enterprise Delivery Control Plane that connects idea, architecture, release, production, automation, governance, and realized value. Unlike another system of work, it orchestrates the seams and preserves accountable decisions.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Category:** The Enterprise Delivery Control Plane
2. **Promise:** From idea to impact. Governed by design.
3. **Product line:** Plan with precision. Launch with confidence.
4. **Proof:** Four delivery pillars. Two cross-cutting capabilities. One operating model.

## Voice

- **Precise:** name the decision, evidence, owner, and next action.
- **Calm:** communicate risk without alarmism.
- **Direct:** prefer short statements and active verbs.
- **Accountable:** distinguish fact, forecast, hypothesis, and target.
- **Welcoming:** explain specialized terms when they first appear.

Avoid exaggerated certainty, unexplained acronyms, decorative aviation language, and savings claims without an observed financial mechanism.

## Visual direction

The visual system combines a dark operational canvas with warm white, signal teal, beacon amber, informational blue, and blocking coral. Alignment rails, fine grid lines, and restrained status lights evoke the operating surface without using aircraft illustration.

## Accessibility

Color never carries meaning alone. All critical states have labels. Text and controls target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Keyboard focus is persistent and visible. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Charts have textual summaries.

## Claim policy

Public numbers must be labeled **observed**, **estimated**, **modeled**, **hypothesis**, or **target**. “Time saved” is capacity released until the organization demonstrates how that capacity was redeployed or converted to a cashable financial outcome.
