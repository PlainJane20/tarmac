# 04 — The modern ITPMO

## From reporting office to operating system

A traditional ITPMO often spends disproportionate effort collecting status, reconciling spreadsheets, preparing meetings, and chasing approvals. TARMAC reframes the function around portfolio flow, decision quality, governance, and measurable outcomes.

## The shift

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| collecting status | identifying the next portfolio intervention |
| project-by-project reporting | cross-portfolio flow and dependency management |
| static gate checklists | versioned policy evaluated against current evidence |
| approval chasing | explicit decision ownership and service levels |
| financial plan variance alone | delivery, production, and benefit evidence together |
| annual governance design | observable controls and expiring exceptions |
| post-hoc audit preparation | retained provenance and decision history |

## ITPMO accountabilities

The modern ITPMO owns the portfolio operating rhythm, lifecycle model, metric definitions, decision-flow health, and value-review discipline. It does not take architecture, security, release, finance, or sponsor accountability away from those designated owners.

## Decision rights

| Decision | Accountable | TARMAC role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accept demand into assessment | ITPMO lead | completeness, scoring, routing, and rationale |
| Approve solution direction | Enterprise/solution architect | evidence packet, findings, ADR, and conditions |
| Accept material security risk | Designated risk owner | risk scope, compensating control, expiry, audit |
| Authorize production release | Release authority | readiness packet, blockers, approvals, and waiver state |
| Close a benefit | Executive sponsor/benefit owner | baseline, target, actual, confidence, and variance |

## Executive experience

The executive command center should not reproduce every work item. It should surface:

- decisions outside SLA;
- material risks without a funded response;
- dependencies threatening multiple commitments;
- release blockers and expiring waivers;
- production signals changing investment assumptions;
- benefits without current actuals; and
- capacity/investment scenarios with explicit confidence.

## Adoption approach

Start with one representative value stream and a small number of decisions. Establish the current baseline before automation. Connect only evidence needed for those decisions. Measure whether TARMAC reduces reconstruction effort and decision latency without increasing administrative burden. Expand only when the pilot proves the operating model.

## Anti-patterns

- centralizing every decision in the ITPMO;
- ranking teams against one another with context-free metrics;
- requiring duplicate data entry already available from an authoritative source;
- treating a governance score as a substitute for a blocking finding;
- presenting modeled capacity release as cash savings; and
- adding AI before evidence quality, authorization, and accountability are defined.
