# 02 — Why the name TARMAC

## The metaphor

Every successful flight begins on the tarmac. Before movement, independent teams coordinate sequencing, safety, readiness, evidence, and clearance. No single team owns the entire operation, but the journey depends on their decisions working together.

Enterprise technology delivery has the same coordination problem. Strategy, architecture, engineering, security, release, operations, finance, and compliance all contribute to a safe and valuable outcome. TARMAC gives those decisions one operating surface.

The metaphor is used with restraint. TARMAC is about alignment, readiness, controlled movement, monitoring, and feedback—not decorative aircraft imagery or aviation puns.

## The acronym

### T — Triage

Turn demand into an explainable portfolio decision. Clarify the outcome, sponsor, urgency, feasibility, risk, baseline, and minimum evidence before investing significant delivery capacity.

### A — Architecture

Turn intent into an approved solution direction. Resolve material requirements, reuse opportunities, integration and data choices, security and privacy risk, non-functional needs, and architectural decisions before commitment becomes expensive.

### R — Release

Turn completed work into an evidence-backed production decision. Evaluate quality, security, operations, change, support, approval, and exception readiness without allowing a percentage to hide a blocker.

### M — Monitoring

Turn production and business signals into accountable action. Observe service health, incidents, adoption, control drift, benefit actuals, and the next portfolio intervention.

### A — Automation & CI/CD

Accelerate every pillar with repeatable evidence collection, deterministic rules, orchestration, notifications, pipeline signals, reconciliation, and safe write-back.

### C — Compliance & Governance

Assure every pillar with decision rights, policy, separation of duties, approvals, exceptions, evidence provenance, retention, and audit history.

## Why the last two are cross-cutting

Automation is not a stage after Monitoring; it changes how Triage, Architecture, Release, and Monitoring operate. Compliance is not a final gate; policy and accountability begin at intake and continue through outcome review. Modeling them as cross-cutting prevents late automation and late compliance from becoming afterthoughts.

## Messaging shorthand

> **TARMAC is where enterprise delivery gets aligned, cleared, released, observed, automated, and governed.**

The shorter public promise is:

> **From idea to impact. Governed by design.**
